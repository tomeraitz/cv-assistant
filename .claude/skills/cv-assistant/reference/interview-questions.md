# Interview question bank (Phases A and B)

Rules:
- One topic per turn. Ask only the questions whose answers are missing from the draft / history.md.
- Ask for numbers (team size, users, %, latency, revenue, time saved). If the user has no number, record the result without one. Never invent, round up, or estimate a number yourself.
- Offer choices where possible (AskUserQuestion) and always allow free text.
- Keep each turn to at most 3 questions on the same topic. Phase A: at most 5 turns in total.

## Topic: Role (per position — B1 / A)
- Company name and your exact title there?
- Start and end month/year? (or "Present")
- What did the role own? Product, system, or area?
- Team: how many people, and were you an IC, lead, or manager? Who did you report to?

## Topic: Results (per position)
- Name 2–3 outcomes you would defend in an interview.
- For each: what changed, measured how (number, %, time, money, users)?
- What did you personally do to cause it (the "by doing Z" part)?
- Any launch, migration, or incident you drove end-to-end?

## Topic: Stack (per position / project)
- Languages and frameworks you used daily?
- Cloud, data stores, infra, CI/CD?
- AI/ML tooling (models, orchestration, vector stores, eval) if relevant?
- Anything you introduced to the team?

## Topic: Update to an existing role (B2)
- Which role changed? (list the `###` blocks from history.md)
- New title and the month it took effect?
- New results since the last update — numbers?
- New stack or responsibilities?

## Topic: Remove a role (B3)
- Which role? (list the `###` blocks)
- Confirm deletion from history.md and the resume; it cannot be restored except from the backup PDF.

## Topic: Education
- Degree, field, institution, years?
- Certifications (issuer, year)?
- Anything notable: thesis, honors, relevant coursework?

## Topic: Target titles (A / B4)
- Which job titles are you applying for now? (up to 3)
- Keep the previous titles as secondary, or drop them?
- Which skills or themes should the summary foreground for these titles?
- Seniority and work type (senior/staff, full-time/contract, remote/hybrid)?

## Topic: Languages
- Spoken languages and level (native / fluent / professional / basic)?

## Topic: Projects (optional)
- Side projects or open-source work worth listing? Year, stack, outcome or link?
